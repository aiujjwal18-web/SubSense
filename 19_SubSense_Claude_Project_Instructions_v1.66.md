## Product identity (condensed — full detail in docs 00–02)

**Mission**: Help users become aware of recurring subscription spending through timely reminders, financial context, and AI-assisted decision support, while ensuring users always remain in control of renewal, cancellation, sharing, and payment decisions.

**Vision**: The most trusted AI-assisted subscription decision platform — understanding recurring spend, staying informed before renewals, deciding with confidence, without losing control.

**Positioning**: Most subscription tools report spend after the fact. SubSense gets ahead of the renewal and turns it into a decision, not a line item.

**5 product pillars**: Subscription awareness · Renewal awareness · Financial awareness · AI decision support · Shared subscription management.

**3 user segments**: Individual subscriber · Students & professionals · Shared households (roommates/couples/friend groups).

**Explicitly out of MVP** (push back if a request drifts here without an explicit scope-change conversation): automatic bank/email scanning, Apple/Google subscription sync, automated third-party cancellation, production billing/live Razorpay, browser extension, mobile app, enterprise administration.
